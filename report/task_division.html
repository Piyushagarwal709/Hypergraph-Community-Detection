1. Project Summary & Core Research Contribution (What We Did)

Identified Research Gap: The original FMHMO algorithm (Xiao et al., IEEE TFS 2025) calculates continuous fuzzy membership degrees u(v, C) between nodes and hyperedge communities, but discards multi-community structure by applying a strict argmax (Eq. 12), forcing a crisp single-community partition.

Our Proposed Solution: We created FMHMO-Overlap, retaining the full continuous membership matrix U ∈ [0, 1]N × K and introducing fuzzy assignment rules to detect genuine multi-community overlapping nodes without any re-training overhead.

Key Technical Milestones Achieved:

• Phase 1 Exact Reproduction (E0): Replicated published paper results (3-decimal match on Workspace: NMI 0.836, ARI 0.849, F1 0.924).

• Continuous Extraction & Rules (Phase 2-3): Implemented 5 assignment rules (R0 argmax baseline, R1 relative, R2 absolute, R3 knee, R4 top-k).

• Planted Benchmark & Metrics (Phase 4-5): Synthetic hypergraph generator with planted overlap (pov), ONMI, Omega Index, and Overlapping F1 evaluation.
